# My Favorite Series: Reacher

## About the Series

**Reacher** is an action and crime thriller television series based on the novels written by *Lee Child*. It follows Jack Reacher, a former military police investigator who travels around the country and gets involved in dangerous situations.

## Main Character

- **Name:** Jack Reacher
- **Actor:** Alan Ritchson
- **Occupation:** Former Military Police Investigator
- **Personality:** Intelligent, strong, brave, and independent.

## Why I Like Reacher

I like Reacher because it has exciting action scenes, interesting mysteries, and unexpected twists. I also enjoy how Jack Reacher uses his intelligence and fighting skills to solve problems and defeat criminals.
